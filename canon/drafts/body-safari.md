## Blueprint

- **Subject:** the body lying in bed, crossed as a night safari — a small land with its own
  cave, mountain, jungle, river and plains, and the creatures that live in each, all
  settling for the night.
- **Environment:** a small, warm land as night comes down over it. **Home:** the land.
- **Guiding thread:** the **safari trail** — named in the second line, it enters at the Wind
  Cave, crosses the land to the edge of the map, and returns the same way to the mountain.
- **Anchor:** the night breeze — in and out of its cave, lifting the mountain. It opens the
  trail, opens the gather, and is the last thing moving before the drum.
- **The setting is carried by inhabitants, not similes.** The original built its safari out
  of comparisons ("like frogs whispering," "like great safari animals at dusk"). Here the
  same creatures simply live in the land: the **finger monkeys** on the Arm Branches, the
  **frogs** of the Tummy Jungle, the **great animals** lying down in the tall grass of the
  Leg Plains, the **toe explorers** camped at the edge of the map, and the hidden drum
  keeping watch over all of it. No similes anywhere in the score.
- **Protect the spell:** each creature does only what that part of a sleepy body truly does
  — a finger still moving, a stomach gurgling, heavy legs, a toe twitching, the belly
  beginning to rise with the chest, the breath slowing, air warmer going out than coming in.
- **Earned language:** two rhetorical questions, both in the second half, separated by the
  drum — *"Is the jungle quiet tonight... or murmuring softly to itself?"* and *"Can you
  feel them curling?"* One long line on the soft break — the drum *"has played since the
  very first morning, / and never missed a beat."*
- **Three passes:** *Introduce* (the trail: cave → mountain → branches and monkeys → drum →
  jungle and frogs → river → plains and great animals → explorers) · *Deepen* (back from the
  edge of the map: the great animals grown heavy; the river deep in its bed; the jungle now
  rising with the mountain; the drum's long history; the monkeys all curled; the mountain
  slower) · *Whole* (gather in echoed 2.5s beats → 12s → seen together → breeze, mountain,
  animals, drum → trademark close with the descent).

## What changed from the original

- **Opening.** The five-line preamble ("a safari unlike any other… closer than the forest…
  Our journey begins.") is gone. The first line is the land at nightfall; the second names
  the safari trail.
- **Questions.** Twelve → two.
- **Similes.** Eleven → none; their animals and tall grass are kept as inhabitants.
- **Captions cut.** "sweet fingers," "all of it working together, all of it safe," "Quiet
  now," "as if it has discovered secrets inside," "digesting the day's adventures."
- **Lines.** No line over 12 words except the drum's, which is split at the comma. The
  original's longest ran past 30.
- **Structure.** One pass plus a list → three passes, a proper gather, and the closing
  descent on *"And the whole quiet safari drifts into the soft, deep night."*

## If the render comes in long or short

- **Long (past ~8:00):** cut *"The day's running and jumping is over."* first, then merge
  the wiggling monkey into one line, then drop the explorers' line from the gather.
- **Short:** deepen, never pad — a return to the explorers at the turn (the one that
  twitched now lying with the rest), or a longer hold after the drum's long line. No third
  question.
