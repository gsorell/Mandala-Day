## Blueprint

- **Subject:** a warm night rain, easing — a world that gathers, holds, and lets go on its
  own schedule.
- **Environment:** the underside of a small roof at night: the eave, one open window, a
  broad leaf below, a puddle at the foot of the steps, wet stone. Rain is the one element
  no shipped kids' piece has claimed, and it rhymes with the breath without being bent to.
- **Home:** the little window standing open under the deep eave. A *resting gaze* (like
  The Play Fort / The Firefly Meadow) — the listener stays; the rain comes to them.
- **Anchor:** **the low corner of the eave**, where the rain runs together and one drop
  swells, hangs, and lets go. Returned to in every pass and in the close.
- **Guiding thread:** the rain easing — a steady hush over the whole roof thinning to a
  few slow taps, until the clouds part.
- **Six recurring elements:** the eave's gathering drop (anchor — gathers · hangs · lets
  go) · the rain over the whole roof (the long hush; loudens in a gust, softens again) ·
  **one drop on the window glass** (slides, joins another, is gone; a new one begins at the
  top and runs the path the last one made — the intimate hide-and-seek) · **the broad
  green leaf** (fills, bends lower, tips, springs back and stands) · the puddle at the
  steps (rings spread, cross, shiver, smooth away) · **the little brown toad** on the wet
  stone (the steady one, present from the first pass).
- **The mirror, kept implicit (Vol I):** the drop gathering, hanging, and releasing is the
  breath; the rings spreading and smoothing are thoughts arising and passing; the drop on
  the glass appearing and vanishing is the peek-a-boo that *is* the care; the roof's hush
  thinning to silence is the settling toward sleep. None named.
- **The return-and-settle beat:** here it belongs to the *weather*, not a second creature.
  The clouds thin, the moon shows low and pale, every wet stone begins to shine, and the
  last drop at the eave holds that light before it falls — the world revealing itself
  exactly as it goes quiet. The toad is still there, settled lower, unmoved throughout.
- **Earned language, on budget (Vol IV):** one rhetorical question — "Can you hear how
  quiet the roof has become?" (final third; rides the real change the rain just made;
  unanswered → 6s silence; and it is the one question in the set that points at *sound*) —
  and one metaphor — the last drop holding the moonlight "like a tiny bead of glass"
  (late, simple, hooked to something a child knows). Non-adjacent.
- **Three passes:** *Introduce* (meet each element once — eave, glass, leaf, puddle, toad)
  · *Deepen* (the whole roof's hush and a gust; the drops quicken and fall into one small
  hollow; the new drop follows the old drop's path; the leaf fills a second time; the
  rings cross and go smooth; the toad settles lower) · *Whole* (the rain thins → the
  question → the clouds part, the stones shine, the last drop holds the light → the
  metaphor → gather in echoed 2.5s beats → 12s immersion → return to the opening image →
  trademark close with the caesura).

top." / "It runs down the path the last one made."), and bring the four `7s` dwells and the
three `8s` holds in the Deepen pass down one step. Never cut the gather, the question, the
metaphor, or the final cadence.
